# UX Agents Manifest

The UX suite includes these specialist agents. Each one has been written against `AGENT_RUBRIC.md`.

- `ux-researcher.md` — Senior UX research lead for evidence quality, research planning, usability testing, discovery, synthesis, and validation strategy.
- `ux-personas.md` — Senior persona and behavioral modeling specialist for turning research into evidence-based personas, JTBD profiles, segments, and design implications.
- `ux-heuristics.md` — Senior usability evaluation specialist for heuristic reviews, task friction diagnosis, interaction clarity, and severity-rated recommendations.
- `ux-accessibility.md` — Senior accessibility and inclusive design specialist for WCAG-informed UX review, assistive technology risk, and practical remediation guidance.
- `ux-product-strategy.md` — Principal-level product strategy partner for opportunity framing, product direction, prioritization, roadmap logic, metrics, and executive-ready UX recommendations.
- `ux-design-systems.md` — Senior design systems specialist for component quality, token structure, UI consistency, accessibility foundations, documentation, governance, and product-scale UX quality.
- `ux-content-strategist.md` — Senior UX content and product writing specialist for clarity, hierarchy, labels, forms, empty states, errors, onboarding copy, and decision-support content.
- `ux-information-architect.md` — Senior information architecture specialist for navigation, taxonomy, wayfinding, hierarchy, search, filtering, labeling, and mental-model alignment.
- `ux-conversion.md` — Senior conversion UX specialist for signup, purchase, demo, lead, checkout, pricing, and task-completion funnels.
- `ux-interaction-designer.md` — Senior interaction design specialist for states, feedback, affordances, flow logic, microinteractions, edge cases, and error prevention.
- `ux-service-designer.md` — Senior service design specialist for end-to-end journeys, backstage operations, cross-channel handoffs, support experiences, and service breakdowns beyond the screen.
- `ux-analytics.md` — Senior UX analytics specialist for interpreting funnels, events, behavioral data, search logs, support signals, surveys, experiments, and product metrics.
- `ux-enterprise.md` — Senior enterprise UX specialist for complex B2B workflows, roles, permissions, admin tools, tables, bulk actions, governance, and power-user productivity.
- `ux-onboarding.md` — Senior onboarding and activation specialist for first-run experiences, setup flows, product education, lifecycle guidance, and early value realization.
- `ux-dashboard.md` — Senior dashboard and decision-support UX specialist for data visualization, tables, filters, KPIs, reporting workflows, and insight-to-action interfaces.
- `ux-commerce.md` — Senior ecommerce UX specialist for product discovery, product detail pages, comparison, cart, checkout, trust, returns, merchandising, and post-purchase experience.
