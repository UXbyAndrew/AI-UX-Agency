# UX Interview Guide

**Study:** [Name]  
**Participant Segment:** [Segment]  
**Session Length:** [Time]  
**Moderator:** [Name]

## Moderator Notes

- Ask open-ended questions.
- Avoid leading language.
- Probe for real behavior, not hypotheticals.
- Capture examples, workarounds, triggers, and decision criteria.

## 1. Introduction

- Thank participant.
- Explain purpose at a high level.
- Confirm consent to record, if applicable.
- Remind them there are no wrong answers.

## 2. Warm-Up

1. Tell me about your role and the type of work you do.
2. How often do you deal with [problem/task/product area]?
3. What tools or processes do you currently use?

## 3. Current Behavior

1. Walk me through the last time you [task].
2. What triggered the need?
3. What steps did you take?
4. Where did things feel easy or difficult?
5. What workarounds did you use?

## 4. Goals and Motivations

1. What are you ultimately trying to accomplish?
2. What does a successful outcome look like?
3. What would make this process feel trustworthy or efficient?

## 5. Pain Points and Barriers

1. What slows you down?
2. What feels confusing or uncertain?
3. What mistakes are easy to make?
4. What information do you wish you had earlier?

## 6. Decision Criteria

1. How do you decide which option to choose?
2. What makes you confident enough to move forward?
3. What would make you stop, delay, or ask for help?

## 7. Concept / Product Reactions

[Use only if showing a product, prototype, or concept.]

1. What is your first impression?
2. What do you think this is for?
3. What would you do next?
4. What feels unclear?
5. What would you expect to happen if you clicked [element]?

## 8. Wrap-Up

1. What is one thing you would improve?
2. Is there anything important we did not ask about?
3. May we follow up if needed?
